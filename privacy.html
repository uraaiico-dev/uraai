Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Welcome to Uraai ("we", "our", or "us"), operated at https://uraai.in. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparent handling of your data when using our AI WhatsApp Bot Builder services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide automated messaging and AI chatbot services:

  • Account Details: Full name, business name, email address, phone number, and subscription plan status.
  • WhatsApp & Meta API Data: WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID), Phone Number ID, access tokens, and customer chat logs for automated AI reply processing.
  • Usage Data: Message counts, lead analytics, and interaction timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To power AI-driven responses to your customers' WhatsApp enquiries.
  • To display live chat logs and lead statistics inside your Uraai Live Inbox.
  • To process payment subscriptions and manage plan limitations.

3. Data Protection & Security

We enforce strict database Row Level Security (RLS) policies on Supabase. Messages sent via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API are end-to-end encrypted in transit. We do not sell or monetize customer chat data or personal details to third parties.

4. User Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account, remove connected WhatsApp accounts, or request permanent deletion of your business data, please contact our support team at support@uraai.in or click Log Out & Delete Account in your dashboard. Data deletion is processed within 48 hours.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, please contact us at support@uraai.in or visit uraai.in.
